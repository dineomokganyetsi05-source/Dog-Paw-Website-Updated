🐾 Dog Paw NPO Website

About Dog Paw

Dog Paw is a non-profit organisation established in 2020 by animal lovers who wanted to make a difference in the lives of dogs. The organisation supports dogs and pet owners in need by promoting animal welfare and providing assistance to the community.

Based in Boksburg, Dog Paw primarily serves the East Rand and hopes to expand its services to the West Rand in the future.

Website Purpose

The purpose of the Dog Paw website is to provide information about the organisation, raise awareness about animal welfare, and make it easier for the community to learn about its services and get involved.

Website Features

* Home: Introduces Dog Paw and its mission.
* About Us: Explains the organisation’s background, goals, and service areas.
* Services: Provides information about the assistance offered to dogs and pet owners.
* Dog Care: Shares useful information about responsible pet ownership and dog welfare.
* Success Stories: Highlights stories about dogs that have received help and support.
* Donations: Provides information on how people can contribute to the organisation.
* Request Help: Allows pet owners to enquire about assistance.
* Contact Us: Provides contact information and ways to reach the organisation.

Technologies Used

* HTML5 – for the structure and content of the website.
* CSS3 – for styling, layout, colours, and responsive design.
* JavaScript – for interactive website functionality.
* Leaflet.js – for the interactive service-area map.
* OpenStreetMap – for map data and geographical information.

Location and Service Areas

Dog Paw is based at 56 Caresbee Road, Mapleton AH, Boksburg, and primarily serves the East Rand. The organisation hopes to expand its services to the West Rand in the future.

The website includes an interactive map to help visitors understand the organisation’s general service areas.

Design and User Experience

The website uses a clean, accessible layout with a teal colour scheme to create a welcoming and consistent visual identity. Its navigation allows visitors to explore the organisation’s information, learn about dog care, read success stories, and find ways to request help or contribute.

Project Objectives

* Promote animal welfare and responsible pet ownership.
* Raise awareness of Dog Paw’s mission and services.
* Provide helpful dog-care information.
* Share inspiring animal welfare stories.
* Encourage community participation, donations, and support.
* Make information about the organisation easily accessible online.
