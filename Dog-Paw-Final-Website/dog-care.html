Dog care information

Simple reminders to help owners provide safe, responsible care for their dogs.

Showing all topics
🥣

Feeding and water

Provide suitable food for your dog's age and needs, and keep clean drinking water available. Ask a veterinary professional about individual dietary concerns.

🧼

Hygiene and grooming

Keep your dog's living area clean and dry. Groom regularly and use products intended for dogs.

🩺

Health and veterinary care

Arrange routine veterinary care and seek professional help when your dog appears injured or unwell.

🦴

Exercise and enrichment

Offer age-appropriate activity, play, rest, and safe opportunities to explore.

🏡

Shelter and safety

Provide a safe resting place, protect dogs from extreme weather, and ensure they cannot wander into danger.

❤️

Responsible ownership

Use patient, humane handling and seek reputable guidance for training and behaviour concerns.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if I cannot afford dog food?

Contact Dog Paw through the enquiry form to ask whether support is available. Availability is not guaranteed.

Can online information replace a veterinarian?

No. These tips are general information and are not a diagnosis. Consult a qualified veterinary professional for medical concerns.

Can I volunteer or donate supplies?

Yes, you can enquire about volunteering or donations using the enquiry or contact form. Confirm current needs before making arrangements.
