Who we are

About Dog Paw

A community-led non-profit focused on supporting dogs and the people who care for them.

Our Story

Dog Paw was established in 2020 during the COVID-19 lockdown by a group of animal lovers. It began with a desire to assist abandoned and stray dogs struggling to survive. The organisation has since developed a focus on helping dog owners who love their pets but cannot always afford essential expenses.

Our Mission

To support dogs and the people who care for them by providing essential resources and assistance to owners who cannot afford adequate care.

Our Vision

A community where every dog has access to food, shelter, basic necessities, and appropriate medical care, regardless of its owner's financial circumstances.

Where We Work: Dog Paw NPO is based in Boksburg and primarily serves the East Rand. The organisation hopes to expand its services to the West Rand in the future. Please contact us to confirm current service availability.

Our Service Areas

Our current base is located at 56 Caresbee Road, Mapleton AH, Boksburg. We primarily serve the East Rand and aim to support dogs and pet owners in need within our community.

We also hope to extend our reach to the West Rand in the future. This is a proposed expansion area, and a West Rand office has not yet been confirmed.

Select a map marker for more information. The Boksburg marker represents Dog Paw's current base. The West Rand marker represents a proposed future service area, not an established office. Map markers may indicate approximate locations.
