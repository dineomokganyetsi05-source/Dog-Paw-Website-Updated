Who we are

About Dog Paw

A community-led non-profit focused on supporting dogs and the people who care for them.

Our story

Dog Paw was established in 2020 during the COVID-19 lockdown by a group of animal lovers. It began with a desire to assist abandoned and stray dogs struggling to survive. The organisation has since developed a focus on helping dog owners who love their pets but cannot always afford essential expenses.

Our mission

To support dogs and the people who care for them by providing essential resources and assistance to owners who cannot afford adequate care.

Our vision

A community where every dog has access to food, shelter, basic necessities, and appropriate medical care, regardless of its owner's financial circumstances.

Where we work: Dog Paw is based in Boksburg and primarily serves the East Rand, with plans to expand into the West Rand. Please contact us to confirm current service availability.
