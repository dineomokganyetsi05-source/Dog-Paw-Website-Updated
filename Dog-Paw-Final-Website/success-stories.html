A True Success Story

A Second Chance at Life

One week before payday, my dog was attacked by another dog in my neighbourhood. The attack left him with a serious open wound on his neck, which had become infected. I was extremely worried because I did not have pet insurance and could not afford the immediate veterinary expenses.

Not knowing where else to turn, I contacted Dog Paw NPO through social media and asked for help. They responded promptly and asked me to send pictures of my dog and explain the condition he was in.

After learning about the severity of the injury, Dog Paw NPO quickly referred me to a veterinary hospital they work with. I explained that I had not received my salary yet, but promised to contribute towards the medical expenses as soon as I got paid.

The veterinarian who operated on my dog was incredibly kind and understanding. My dog received the treatment he needed and was cared for until I was able to collect him.

Today, my dog is alive and healthy. I will always be grateful to Dog Paw NPO and the veterinary team for helping us during such a difficult time. Without their support, I do not know whether my dog would have survived.

This experience showed me how much a helping hand can mean during an emergency. Thank you, Dog Paw NPO, for giving my dog a second chance at life.

Outcome: A healthy dog and a grateful owner.
